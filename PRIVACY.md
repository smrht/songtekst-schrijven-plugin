# Privacy

Songtekst schrijven is a skills package by Samautomation. It has no server connection, account, analytics or telemetry. The bundled Python checker reads the local text file you give it, writes a report to standard output and makes no network requests. We do not receive your lyrics or details through this package.

Your AI host (for example Claude or ChatGPT) processes your conversation under its own privacy policy. Personal details you share for a song, such as names and anecdotes, stay in that conversation.

Support: https://github.com/smrht/songtekst-schrijven-plugin/issues. Do not post private details in a public issue.
